# TrueProfit drafting format

Team format for full articles drafted from an approved outline. Khanh's instructions for a specific article override these defaults.

## Outline lock

- Use only the H1/H2/H3 headings in the outline. Keep their wording, levels and order; fix only obvious typos. Don't add, remove, rename, merge or split headings, and don't invent H3s.
- Keep existing numbering as it is; don't number unnumbered H3s.
- `(area to win)` is an outline marker. Ask Khanh once per article whether to keep it in the draft heading; earlier Final tabs kept it.
- If a locked heading or title conflicts with intent or lacks the keyword, keep it and flag it in the handoff note.

## Length and depth

- About 1,500–2,000 words.
- The first two H2s after the introduction carry the core intent and get the most depth. Other H2s stay concise and focused.

## Meta description

At the top, max 155 characters, includes the main keyword naturally, summarizes the real promise. No quotation marks or unnecessary special characters.

## Introduction

Three short paragraphs: (1) answers the first H2's main question in up to 30 words; (2) answers the second H2's key idea in up to 30 words; (3) says what readers will get from the article. Main keyword appears early and naturally.

## Quick Recap

Right after the introduction, 4–5 distinct takeaways in complete natural sentences, each answering a major article question. Use a bold paragraph label if the outline has no Quick Recap heading.

## Lists and tables

- Prefer connected prose for reasoning. Bullets for steps or simple parallel lists, introduced by one short sentence (up to about 15 words). Body lists are bulleted, not numbered.
- Each action bullet carries its short reason; no bare commands.
- Tables for comparisons. Product or business-idea listicles open with a summary table (Product | Estimated Selling Price | Supplier Price | Gross Profit Margin); never fabricate prices or margins to fill it, keep gross and net margin distinct, and label estimates.

## Links

- Internal anchors vary naturally and stay close to the destination's intent; keep meaningful qualifiers and the action verb when useful. A verified benchmark figure can be the anchor. Don't repeat one exact-match anchor.
- No external links in the body; 3–5 clean external reference links at the end under a bold **References** label if the outline has no heading for them.
- Further Reading: a simple `Further Reading:` label followed by related URLs on separate lines, never a table.
- Free tools: a plain `Content Highlight` line on its own, followed by a `Pro Tips: Use our free ...` paragraph with a natural link and any relevant limitation.

## Benchmarks

Prefer TrueProfit data. Never invent internal numbers; use sparse `[Insert TrueProfit internal benchmark ...]` placeholders only where they genuinely improve credibility. Give every external statistic its source, date and scope.

## FAQs

Only when the outline has an FAQ heading. Keep every existing FAQ question as written and in order; if one repeats a heading or the body, keep it while drafting and flag it in the handoff note. Answers are direct and brief with one useful clarification.

## Uniqueness

Write original synthesis. Never claim a uniqueness or plagiarism score without running an actual check.
